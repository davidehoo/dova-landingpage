How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Dova. https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed phones; how do I get my subscription back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID. If access doesn't return, email us.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us from the address on your account and we'll take care of it. See the Privacy Policy.

Found a bug or have feedback?

Email us with your device model and iOS version, plus a screenshot if you can.
