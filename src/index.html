Christian faith, a few minutes a day

A daily path to know God.

{{brand}} helps you learn the Christian faith in a few quiet minutes a day. Follow today's step with {{mascot}} the dove: a little Scripture, a little reflection, a little prayer.

How it works

Three steps, then the day is done.

{{brand}} keeps each day small enough to finish.

  1. 1

    Open the day

    {{mascot}} meets you at the next step. One path is waiting.

  2. 2

    Take the step

    Read a short passage, sit with a question, and pray in words you can actually say.

  3. 3

    Mark it complete

    Keep the streak, close the app, and leave the rest of the day to itself.

Features

Made for days you can keep.

  • Today's journey

    Read, reflect, and pray, in order. When the step is done, the day is done.

  • Short lessons

    Each piece is written for a phone and a few quiet minutes.

  • A kind streak

    The streak and a little XP show the days you returned. The path stays open when a day gets away from you.

  • A dove named {{mascot}}

    Round glasses, a gold halo, and a calm nudge toward the next step.

FAQ

A few short answers.

What is {{brand}}?

{{brand}} is a mobile app for learning about God. Each day is a guided step through Scripture, reflection, and prayer.

Who is {{mascot}}?

{{mascot}} is the dove who comes with you. In the Christian faith the dove is a sign of the Holy Spirit and of peace.

How long is a day, and do I need to know the Bible?

Most steps take a few minutes. You can begin with no background at all. The path starts simple and builds.

Is this for one church?

{{brand}} is rooted in Scripture and the broad Christian faith, and it is written to be at home across many traditions.

Can I download it yet?

Not yet. The apps are still on the way. The terms already explain how a download will work.
